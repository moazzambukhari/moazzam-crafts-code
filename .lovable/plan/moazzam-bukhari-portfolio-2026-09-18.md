# Moazzam Bukhari Portfolio

## Goal
Build a premium, dark-first single-page portfolio for recruiters and international remote opportunities, following the selected frosted-glass direction while preserving every requested section and interaction.

## What I’ll build
- A compact glass navigation bar with mobile menu, active-section state, theme control, and smooth section links.
- A strong first screen with Moazzam’s positioning, animated specialty line, resume/work/contact actions, social placeholders, and credibility metrics.
- Full About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Services, Why Work With Me, Resume, and Contact sections using the provided factual content only.
- Seven filterable project cards with technology labels, detail dialogs, and clearly disabled placeholder links where URLs were not provided.
- Form validation, scroll reveals, animated statistics, back-to-top control, reduced-motion support, and keyboard-accessible interactions.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile with semantic structure and complete page metadata.

## Visual direction
- Deep charcoal surfaces with restrained cyan and periwinkle accents.
- Space Grotesk headings and Inter body typography.
- Selective frosted-glass panels, subtle grid texture, crisp borders, and calm motion.
- No generic stock portrait or fabricated likeness; the profile visual will be an abstract, engineered software-workspace image until a real portrait is provided.

## Technical notes
- Keep the required TanStack project foundation while implementing all behavior locally in the page without backend services.
- Use semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet and existing icon tooling for accessible controls.
- The contact form validates locally and opens the visitor’s email client because no receiving email or form service was supplied.
- Resume and social destinations remain explicit placeholders until real files or URLs are provided.

## Verification
- Check the live page at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Test navigation, filtering, dialogs, theme control, form validation, and back-to-top behavior.
- Confirm the preview builds cleanly with no runtime or accessibility regressions.
